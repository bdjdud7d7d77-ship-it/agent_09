### Summary

The book claims that getting rich comes from a system you can calculate, not from luck. Its core formula is wealth = **G (income growth rate) × R (investment return rate)**. Drawing on the author's 30 years of studying economics and investing, it lays out a three-stage roadmap:

1. **Grow:** set a target asset level, then manage income and returns to build assets step by step.
2. **Compound:** use compounding and a practical trading formula to accelerate account growth.
3. **Preserve:** use the "4% rule" after retirement to keep a lasting cash flow.

The promotional copy presents it as a follow-up to *The Millionaire Fastlane*: where that book inspires the dream, this one turns it into concrete numbers.
