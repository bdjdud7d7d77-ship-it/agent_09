# 04. Social and Economic Impact, Ethics, and Regulation

## 1. Economic Impact and Investment

- Global corporate AI investment reached **$581.7 billion**, up 130% year over year; generative AI investment alone rose nearly fivefold to **$170.9 billion** (Stanford AI Index 2026).
- US private AI investment was **$285.9 billion** in 2025, more than 23 times China's $12.4 billion.
- The US had **1,953** newly funded AI companies in 2025 and hosts **5,427** data centers, more than 10 times any other country.

## 2. Impact on Jobs

- **WEF Future of Jobs Report**: by 2030, about **170 million** jobs created and **92 million** displaced, a net gain of **78 million**; this churn affects about 22% of today's jobs. About **59%** of the global workforce will need reskilling by 2030.
- **IMF**: about **40%** of global employment is exposed to AI, rising to about **60%** in advanced economies. About 1 in 10 job vacancies in advanced economies now requires at least one new (often IT/AI) skill.
- Roles at highest risk include telephone operators, insurance claims clerks, and bill collectors; mid-level roles also face pressure.
- **Perception gap**: 73% of experts expect a positive impact of AI on jobs, versus only 23% of the public.

## 3. Safety and Ethical Issues

- Documented AI incidents rose from **233** (2024) to **362** (Stanford AI Index 2026).
- Key concerns: misinformation and deepfakes, bias and discrimination, privacy, copyright of training data, hallucinations, and misuse.
- Safety, policy, and education are falling behind the pace of capability growth.

## 4. Energy and Environment

- IEA: global data-center electricity use is projected to more than double, from about **415 TWh (2024)** to about **945 TWh (2030)**, around 3% of world electricity.
- Electricity used by AI-specific servers is expected to roughly triple by 2030; the US and China account for almost 80% of the increase.

## 5. Regulation

| Jurisdiction | Law | Timeline / Key Points |
|---|---|---|
| EU | **EU AI Act** | General-purpose AI rules from Aug 2025; most provisions apply from Aug 2026; high-risk AI rules from Aug 2027. Risk-based approach. |
| South Korea | **AI Basic Act** (Act on the Development of AI and Establishment of Trust) | Took effect **Jan 22, 2026**; second comprehensive AI law after the EU. Risk assessment for "high-impact" and generative AI; local representative required for foreign firms; at least a one-year grace period before fines. Aims to balance innovation and safety. |
