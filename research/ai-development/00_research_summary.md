## Core Message

AI has moved from a research field marked by boom-and-bust cycles to a general-purpose technology adopted faster than the PC or the internet. Its capabilities keep accelerating, but safety, regulation, energy, education, and workforce transition are lagging behind. The central task for the coming years is closing that gap.
