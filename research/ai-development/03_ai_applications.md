# 03. AI Applications by Industry

## Overall Adoption

- **88%** of organizations worldwide use AI in at least one business function (Stanford AI Index 2026), up from about 55% in 2024.
- Generative AI reached **53%** of the global population within three years, faster than the personal computer or the internet.
- **4 in 5** university students use generative AI.
- The value of generative AI tools to US consumers is estimated at **$172 billion per year** (early 2026).

## By Industry

> Note: Industry-level figures below come from market-research aggregators and vary between sources. Treat them as indicative, not exact.

| Industry | Main Uses | Indicative Figures |
|---|---|---|
| Healthcare | Medical imaging, clinical decision support, administrative automation, drug discovery | Adoption ~62–67% (2026); US FDA cleared ~178 AI medical devices in 2025 (vs. 91 in 2024) |
| Finance | Fraud detection, credit scoring, risk management, customer service | Adoption ~79%; largest share of enterprise AI spending (~$38.2B, ~20.5%) |
| Manufacturing | Predictive maintenance, quality inspection, process optimization | ~77% of manufacturers use AI; AI spending up ~48% year over year |
| Education | Personalized tutoring, grading support, content creation | Lowest sector adoption (~34%) due to budget and policy limits; only half of US middle/high schools have AI policies |
| Software | Code generation, testing, debugging, autonomous coding agents | Tech sector adoption ~88%; SWE-bench scores near 100% |

## Science: AI-Driven Discovery

- **AlphaFold** (DeepMind) solved the 50-year-old protein-structure prediction problem and predicted structures of nearly all ~200 million known proteins.
- Demis Hassabis and John Jumper shared the **2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry** (with David Baker for computational protein design) — the first Nobel for an AI-enabled scientific breakthrough.

## Gap Between Adoption and Value

- High adoption does not always mean high returns; some surveys report that a large share of companies do not yet see clear ROI from AI.
