# 01. History of AI Development

## Key Milestones

| Period | Event | Significance |
|---|---|---|
| 1950 | Alan Turing proposes the "Turing Test" | First framing of the question "Can machines think?" |
| 1956 | Dartmouth Workshop (McCarthy, Minsky, Rochester, Shannon) | The term "Artificial Intelligence" is coined; AI is founded as a field |
| 1974–1980 | First AI Winter | Funding collapses after unmet expectations and limited computing power |
| 1980s | Expert systems boom | Rule-based AI used commercially |
| 1987–1993 | Second AI Winter | Expert systems prove costly and brittle |
| 1997 | IBM Deep Blue defeats Garry Kasparov | AI beats a world chess champion |
| 2012 | AlexNet wins ImageNet (Krizhevsky, Sutskever, Hinton) | Beats runner-up by about 10.8 percentage points; starts the deep learning boom |
| 2016 | DeepMind AlphaGo defeats Lee Sedol | AI masters Go, long thought too complex for machines |
| 2017 | "Attention Is All You Need" introduces the Transformer | Foundation of all modern large language models |
| 2022.11.30 | OpenAI releases ChatGPT | 1M users in 5 days, 100M in about 2 months (fastest-growing consumer app at the time) |
| 2023.03 | GPT-4 released | Large multimodal model accepting image and text input |
| 2024 | Nobel Prizes in Physics (Hopfield, Hinton) and Chemistry (Hassabis, Jumper, Baker) | First Nobel Prizes recognizing AI research and AI-enabled discovery |
| 2024–2025 | Reasoning models (OpenAI o1, DeepSeek-R1, etc.) | Models "think" step by step at inference time |
| 2025–2026 | Rise of AI agents | Shift from answering questions to autonomously completing tasks |

## Paradigm Shifts

1. **Symbolic / rule-based AI (1950s–1980s)**: Humans hand-coded logic and rules.
2. **Machine learning (1990s–2000s)**: Systems learned patterns from data using statistical methods.
3. **Deep learning (2012–)**: Multi-layer neural networks, GPUs, and big data enabled breakthroughs in vision and speech.
4. **Generative AI / foundation models (2017–)**: Transformer-based large models generate text, images, code, and more.
5. **Agentic AI (2025–)**: Models plan, use tools, and carry out multi-step work.

## Key Insight

AI history follows a cycle of "summers" (hype and investment) and "winters" (disappointment and funding cuts). The current boom differs from earlier ones because it rests on three factors arriving at once: massive data, cheap parallel compute (GPUs), and scalable architectures (Transformers).
